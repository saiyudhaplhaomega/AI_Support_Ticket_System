# Resetting your NOAVIA password

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and enter the email address for
your NOAVIA workspace. We send a reset link that expires after 30 minutes.

For security, the link can be used once. If it has expired, request a new
link rather than forwarding an old email. Workspace owners cannot view or set
another person's password.

If the reset email does not arrive, check spam and confirm that the address is
spelled exactly as it appears in the workspace member list. Contact support
with your workspace name and the time of the request; do not send a password
or reset link.
