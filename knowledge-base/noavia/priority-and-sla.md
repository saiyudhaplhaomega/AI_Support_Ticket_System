# Ticket priority and response targets

NOAVIA assigns a priority from the impact and urgency described in a ticket.
P1 means all users cannot process tickets; P2 means a core workflow is
materially degraded; P3 is a limited workaround issue; P4 is a question or
feature request.

The support team targets an initial response within one hour for P1, four
business hours for P2, one business day for P3, and two business days for P4.
Response targets are not guaranteed resolution times.

To help us assess impact, include the workspace name, affected ticket IDs,
when the problem started, and whether a workaround exists. Never include API
keys, passwords, or full payment-card numbers.
