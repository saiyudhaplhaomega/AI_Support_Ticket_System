# Knowledge search and suggested replies

NOAVIA searches approved workspace articles to suggest a reply for a support
ticket. Suggestions are drafts: an agent must verify the facts and edit the
reply before sending it to a customer.

Search results may be empty when no approved article is relevant. In that
case, choose **Escalate for review** and describe what information is missing.
Do not invent policy, refund terms, or troubleshooting steps.

Authors can update an article from Knowledge settings. Newly approved changes
are normally searchable within fifteen minutes. Draft and archived articles
are not included in suggestions.
