# Ticket retention and export

Closed tickets remain available according to the workspace retention setting.
Workspace administrators can request a CSV export from **Workspace settings
> Data management**. Exports are prepared asynchronously and the download
link expires after 24 hours.

Deleting a ticket removes it from the active workspace view but may take up
to 30 days to leave backups. A legal hold can prevent deletion until the hold
is released.

For a retention question, support can explain the configured policy but cannot
make legal determinations. Include the workspace name and the ticket IDs or
date range involved.
