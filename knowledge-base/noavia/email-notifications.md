# Email notification delivery

Workspace notification emails are sent from `notifications@noavia.example`.
An assignee receives a message when a ticket is assigned, escalated, or
reopened unless they have disabled that notification type in Profile settings.

If messages are missing, first check spam or a company quarantine. Then add
the sender to the allow list and ask an administrator to confirm that your
member account is active. Delivery can take up to ten minutes after an event.

For a delivery investigation, provide the ticket ID, recipient domain, and
approximate event time. Do not provide mailbox credentials.
