# Duplicate subscription charge

NOAVIA subscriptions renew on the billing date shown in **Workspace settings
> Billing**. A pending card authorization can appear beside the completed
renewal for up to five business days; it is not a second settled charge.

If two charges have both settled, send support the invoice numbers and the
last four digits of the card. We will compare the transactions and, when a
duplicate is confirmed, return the extra payment to the original method.

Do not open a bank dispute while the review is in progress. A dispute can
temporarily restrict changes to the subscription.
