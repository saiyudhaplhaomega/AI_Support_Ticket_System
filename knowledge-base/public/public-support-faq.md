# NOAVIA public support FAQ

## How do I get help?

Use the support form to describe the issue, what you expected to happen, and any relevant error message. A support request may be reviewed by a person before a final response is sent.

## I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Use the **Forgot password** option on the sign-in page. The reset email is sent to the address associated with the workspace. If it does not arrive, check spam or a company email quarantine first.

## What information should I avoid sending?

Do not send passwords, password-reset links, API keys, access tokens, payment-card details, or mailbox credentials through the public chat or support form.

## Can the public assistant answer every question?

No. The assistant answers only from approved public information. If it cannot find an answer, it will ask you to contact support rather than inventing an answer.
